Questions

 

What exactly do I receive?

14 print-ready PDFs (US Letter + A4), one Excel tracker (.xlsx, works in Google Sheets), 12 Instagram-ready PNGs, 8 preview images, and step-by-step instruction pages.

How do I add my clinic logo?

Upload any PDF to a free Canva account — every heading and label is editable text. Delete the logo placeholder, drop in your logo, export as PDF Print.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. These are administrative organization templates. Aftercare instruction areas are fill-in placeholders for your clinic's own approved wording, and any clinical content should be reviewed by qualified professionals.

Can I share the files with others?

The license covers one business — print unlimited copies for your own clinic, but please don't share or resell the files.

What paper should I print on?

Standard office paper for forms; heavy cardstock for cards. Print at "Actual size" (100%), never "Fit to page".
